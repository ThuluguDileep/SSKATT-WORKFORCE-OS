Multi-Tenant Organization Architecture

• Co-Existing Organization IDs: Multiple Organization IDs can coexist within a single sector domain without overwriting existing data.
• Custom Credentials: Each Organization ID provisions its own unique admin username and password for partitioned access.
• Staff & Attendance Segregation: Staff records, tasks, leaves, and biometric logs remain strictly bound to their respective Organization ID.
